Privacy Policy — Block Tower (Torre de Blocos)

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how the Block Tower app ("the App"), developed by Torre de Blocos, handles user information.

1. Data we collect

The App does not require sign-up and does not directly collect your name, email, phone number or any other personal data. Your score and preferences (such as sound on/off) are stored only on your device.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Device advertising identifier (Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location;
  • Device information (model, operating system, language);
  • Interactions with ads.

This data may be used to show personalized ads, according to your consent. Learn more at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent

Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the App shows a consent message before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time using the "Privacy" button on the App's home screen.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), you have the right to request information about, correction of, or deletion of your data. Since we do not store personal data on our own servers, requests related to ads should also be sent to Google.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version will always be available on this page.
